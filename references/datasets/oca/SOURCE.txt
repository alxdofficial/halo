Overhead Car Assembly IMU Dataset (OCA)

Dataset record: https://doi.org/10.24406/fordatis/121
Publication: https://doi.org/10.1109/INDIN51773.2022.9976078

Verified for application planning on 2026-08-29. The complete 33.22 MB ZIP was downloaded and all
twelve CSV sessions were inspected. It contains timestamps in milliseconds, four accelerometers and
gyroscopes, six action labels, NULL, and official subject-level train/validation/test splits. Sessions
use native rates near either 20 Hz or 27 Hz; `P0-R0.csv` contains an 81-second timestamp gap. The
accelerometer norm is consistent with m/s2. The publication identifies the IMU as a BNO055, and every
released gyroscope value is exactly quantized at the device's 1/16 degree/s output scale. Convert
acceleration by `/ 9.80665` and gyroscope by `* pi / 180`. Data have not been imported into the HALO
representation-training corpus.
The separate application adapter emits 13 quality-contiguous records from the twelve sessions,
preserves all four placements and native rates, applies those unit conversions, and retains the
official split metadata. The training split and validation split share some published participants;
only the official test participants are subject-disjoint from both.
