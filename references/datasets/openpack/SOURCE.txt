The selected acquisition is the non-RGB release. The dataset is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; the repository
documents separate terms for the RGB release. Verified on 2026-08-29: all 21 subject-ID archives
(5.20 GiB) are present, representing 16 distinct people after the five documented aliases are
collapsed. The 416 ATR IMU files contain finite gravity-present acceleration in g and gyroscope in
degree/s. Preserve measured 30.30-33.33 Hz timestamps, split or mask two gaps in
`U0108.zip:atr/atr01/S0400.csv`, and exclude one zero-duration `Bend Flap` annotation in `U0208`.
The application adapter selects the documented `atr01` right-wrist IMU, emits 105 usable
quality-contiguous records, drops one isolated single sample, excludes annotations with no selected
sensor overlap, and clips observable fragments while retaining their original bounds in metadata.
