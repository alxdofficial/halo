Recognition and Repetition Counting for Complex Physical Exercises with Deep Learning

Verified for application planning on 2026-08-29. The selected authored code and sensor release
contains 446 non-NULL exercise arrays and 5,461 non-NULL repetition arrays at the authors'
interpolated approximately 100 Hz rate. Arrays contain wrist and ankle acceleration in m/s2,
gyroscope in rad/s, and orientation. Six repetition fragments last only 0.08-0.16 seconds and must
be excluded or justified. The paper reports 54 participants, while the released map exposes 57
codes, seven of which are NULL-only; 50 codes have constrained-workout data, reconciling the paper's
wider study count. Freeze source-code splits rather than inferring identities. Repetition slices run
from one machine-paced vibration cue to the next and are not observed natural repetition boundaries.
The public Drive release does not contain a clear machine-readable data licence. Confirm permitted
research and publication use before the dataset contributes to a final paper result.
The application adapter exposes all 5,923 authored parent/repetition arrays lazily in canonical
units. Parent exercises and repetition excerpts duplicate signal by design and must be selected as
alternative supervision views, not counted as independent observations in one training split.
